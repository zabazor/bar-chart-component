More Use Cases

You could even have multiple of these percent charts for different project calculations, like materials used. Which could be helpful for figuring out how to price out future projects.

Potential Future Update

Consider exploring other bar chart options. This one clearly needs to be renamed bar-percentage-chart because it is percentaged displayed in a single bar. It should be really easy to make a vertical or horizontal bar-chart option. Even a circle-pie-chart. Line graphs might be a challenge, because they are connecting random vectors, where these other charts are percentage or individual disconnected points, but it could be fun to make a tool that could do all 3 of those drastically different functions. Then explore converting it to other frameworks, since it is currently one that requires angular to work.
